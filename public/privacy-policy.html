Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 17, 2024

Summary: Stick Stretch Path respects your privacy. We collect minimal data necessary to run the game, and we do not sell your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Stick Stretch Path collects the following types of information:

  • Gameplay Data: High scores, coins earned, unlocked items, achievements, and game settings. This data is stored locally on your device.
  • Device Information: Basic device identifiers may be collected by our advertising partners for ad personalization and analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to:

  • Save your game progress and preferences
  • Track achievements and daily challenges
  • Display relevant advertisements
  • Improve the gaming experience

3. Data Storage

Your gameplay data (scores, coins, settings, unlocked items) is stored locally on your device using browser/app local storage. This data is not transmitted to our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data. Google Privacy Policy

5. Advertising

Stick Stretch Path displays advertisements through Google AdMob. These ads help us keep the game free. You may see:

  • Banner ads at the bottom of the screen
  • Interstitial ads between game sessions
  • Rewarded video ads (optional, for in-game rewards)

AdMob may use device identifiers to show personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Stick Stretch Path is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game content is suitable for all ages.

7. Data Security

Since gameplay data is stored locally on your device, the security of this data depends on your device's security settings. We recommend using device-level security features like screen locks.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete your data: Clear your local storage or uninstall the app to remove all game data
  • Opt out of personalized ads: Adjust your device's advertising settings
  • Access your data: Your game progress is visible within the app

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the app's feedback feature or leave a review on the Google Play Store.
